Nature

Natural Attractions

Busra Waterfall

Busra Waterfall

Bousra Waterfall is one of the most famous and beautiful waterfalls in Mondulkiri, located about 35 km from Sen Monorom. Known for its powerful two-tier cascade, it offers stunning views, especially during the rainy season. Surrounded by lush forest, the site is ideal for sightseeing, photography, and relaxing in nature. Bousra is also an important eco-tourism destination that attracts both local and international visitors.

Busra Waterfall
Chrey Thom Waterfall

Chrey Thom Waterfall

Chrey Thom Waterfall is a beautiful natural attraction in Mondulkiri, located about 20–43 km from Sen Monorom. Surrounded by lush forests and plantations, it offers a peaceful environment with fresh air and scenic views. The waterfall changes with the seasons(taller in the dry season and wider in the rainy season)but remains accessible year-round. Visitors can enjoy picnicking, hiking, swimming, and exploring nearby ethnic villages.

Chrey Thom Waterfall
Renaissance Mineral

Renaissance Mineral

The Memang Gold Mines Area is a popular eco-tourism destination in Mondulkiri, located about 45 km from Sen Monorom. Surrounded by forests and scenic landscapes, it offers a peaceful and adventurous experience for visitors. Known for its rich biodiversity, the area is home to various wildlife and plant species. Visitors can enjoy trekking, camping, and nature exploration, making it a great spot for adventure and nature lovers.

Renaissance Mineral
Sen Monorum Waterfall

Sen Monorum Waterfall

Sen Monorom Waterfall is a popular local attraction located about 5 km from Sen Monorom town. Easily accessible by a red soil road lined with rubber, coffee, and cashew plantations, it is a favorite gathering spot for locals during holidays and festivals. The waterfall has three stages, ranging from gentle flows to stronger cascades, offering spaces for relaxing and enjoying nature. With its peaceful setting and close proximity to town, it’s a perfect spot for a quick nature escape in Mondulkiri.

Sen Monorum Waterfall
Jahoo

Jahoo

Jahoo is a community-owned eco-tourism project in Mondulkiri Province, known for its focus on wildlife conservation and immersive nature experiences. Visitors can observe the endangered Southern Yellow-Cheeked Crested Gibbon and other wildlife such as langurs, macaques, and diverse bird species in their natural habitat. The camp supports Indigenous Bunong communities through community-based tourism, offering insights into their culture and forest knowledge. With simple, eco-friendly bamboo accommodations and guided rainforest treks, Jahoo promotes sustainable travel.

Kampong Phluk
Phnum Prech Wildlife Sanctuary

Phnom Prech Sanctuary

Phnom Prech Sanctuary is a popular natural attraction in Mondulkiri, located in the Eastern Plains known for its vast forests and scenic landscapes. It is part of a large conservation area supported by the Cambodian government and the World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF), protecting diverse wildlife. The sanctuary is home to endangered species such as Asian elephants, wild water buffalo, and giant ibises, making it an important site for biodiversity conservation.

Phnom Prech Sanctuary
Phnom Doh Krormom Shrine

Phnom Doh Krormom Shrine

Yok Sros Phlom (Phnom Dos Kramom) is a scenic hill located just 3 km from Sen Monorom. Known for its grassy landscape and gentle slopes, it offers a unique natural view unlike dense forest areas. From the top, visitors can enjoy panoramic views of Mondulkiri town and surrounding mountains. It’s a peaceful spot ideal for sightseeing and relaxing in nature.

Phnom Doh Krormom Shrine
Mondulkiri Project Elephant Sanctuary

Mondulkiri Project Elephant Sanctuary

Meet these benevolent giants at the Elephant Valley Project, an ethical and conservation project that provides a refuge for retired and rescued elephants. Learn about these majestic creatures roaming freely in their natural habitat, learn about their behavior, and participate in activities like feeding and bathing. It is an experience that touches the heart, combining respect for nature and responsible tourism. An unforgettable adventure, one of the things to do in Mondulkiri for all wildlife and nature lovers, not to be missed.

Mondulkiri Project Elephant Sanctuary

Cambodian Taste

Traditional Food

Kralan Mondulkiri

Kralan is a traditional Cambodian snack popular in Mondulkiri, made from sticky rice, black beans, coconut milk, and palm sugar, roasted inside bamboo. This cooking method gives it a smoky aroma and a lightly sweet, rich flavor. Often enjoyed during festivals or as a morning snack, Kralan is commonly sold along village roads. It’s a simple yet meaningful dish that reflects local culture and tradition

Samlor Korko

Samlor Korko is a traditional Cambodian soup popular in Mondulkiri, known for its rich and comforting flavor. It is made with a mix of vegetables, herbs, and a flavorful broth, sometimes with fish, chicken, or pork. Often enjoyed during the rainy season, this nourishing dish reflects the balance of local ingredients and Khmer cooking traditions. It’s a must-try for anyone wanting an authentic taste of Mondulkiri cuisine.

Samlor Prong

Samlor Prong is a unique Bunong dish in Mondulkiri, cooked inside bamboo over a fire. It combines fish, eggplant, herbs, and spices, creating a rich, smoky flavor. More than just food, it reflects the local way of life and connection to nature. This traditional soup is a must-try for an authentic Mondulkiri experience.

Chaa Angrong Sach Ko

Chaa Angrong Sach Ko is a unique Mondulkiri dish made with stir-fried beef, herbs, and red tree ants, giving it a tangy and aromatic flavor. The ants add a natural sourness that blends well with spices like lemongrass, garlic, and chili. Though unusual, it’s a traditional local specialty and a great way to experience authentic Mondulkiri cuisine.
