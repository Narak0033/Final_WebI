Kingdom of Cambodia

Discover Kampot
Province

Kampot is a laid-back riverside province in southern Cambodia famous for its historic colonial architecture, world-renowned pepper plantations, salt fields, and durian.

Explore

Cultural Heritage

Museum

Kampot Provincial Museum

Kampot Provincial Museum is a charming cultural attraction located in a restored French colonial building in the heart of Kampot. The museum offers visitors a fascinating journey through the province’s rich history, from ancient times to the modern era, through informative panels, historical photographs, and archaeological artifacts. With its blend of heritage architecture and educational exhibits, it is an ideal place for travelers who want to gain a deeper appreciation of Kampot’s cultural and historical significance.

Kampot Provincial Museum
Nature & History

Bokor Mountain

Bokor Mountain is a cultural and historical destination in Kampot, known for its French colonial heritage, religious sites, and scenic mountain setting. Visitors can explore landmarks such as the old Bokor Hill Station, abandoned church, Buddhist wat, and royal-era buildings, which reflect Cambodia’s rich history and diverse cultural influences.

Bokor Mountain

Nature

Natural Attractions

Waterfall

Popokvil Waterfall

Popokvil Waterfall is one of Bokor Mountain’s most beautiful natural attractions, known for its refreshing atmosphere and scenic surroundings. Surrounded by lush forest, the waterfall flows in two tiers, creating a peaceful and cool environment that is perfect for relaxing and enjoying nature. During the rainy season, the water flow becomes stronger, making it an especially impressive sight for visitors.

Popokvil Waterfall
Cave & Temple

Phnom Chhngok Cave Temple

Phnom Chhngok Cave Temple is a unique attraction that blends natural beauty with ancient history. Located inside a limestone cave, it features stunning stalactites and a small brick temple dating back to the 7th century. Surrounded by peaceful countryside and rice fields, it offers visitors a quiet and scenic place to explore while learning about Cambodia’s early cultural heritage.

Phnom Chhngok Cave
Salt Fields

Kampot Salt Fields

Kampot Salt Fields are a unique cultural and natural attraction where visitors can witness traditional salt production in Cambodia. Stretching across wide open plains, the salt fields create beautiful reflective landscapes, especially during sunrise and sunset. Local farmers harvest salt by hand using age-old methods, offering visitors a chance to learn about this important local industry and way of life. The Kampot Salt Fields are a must-visit for those interested in culture, photography, and authentic rural experiences.

Kampot Salt Fields
Farm

La Plantation

La Plantation is a famous pepper farm where visitors can discover the world-renowned Kampot pepper and its traditional cultivation. Surrounded by beautiful countryside, the farm offers guided tours that explain the growing process, harvesting methods, and different types of pepper. Visitors can also enjoy pepper tasting experiences and learn why Kampot pepper is highly valued worldwide. La Plantation is a perfect destination for those interested in agriculture, local culture, and authentic experiences in Kampot.

La Plantation
Rapids

Teuk Chhou Rapids

Teuk Chhou Rapids is a popular natural attraction in Kampot, known for its clear flowing water, fresh air, and peaceful surroundings. The shallow rapids create a cool and refreshing spot where visitors can relax, swim, and enjoy nature. With its riverside huts, local food, and scenic greenery, it is an ideal place for a relaxing day out with friends and family.

Teuk Chhou Rapids

Night Life

Kampot Night Market

Kampot Night Market is a lively evening destination where visitors can experience local culture, food, and daily life. The market is filled with street food stalls, small shops, and vendors selling clothes, souvenirs, and handmade items. It is a great place to try Cambodian dishes, enjoy the vibrant atmosphere, and explore the local lifestyle at night. Kampot Night Market is perfect for an evening walk, food tasting, and cultural experience.

Restaurant & Bar

Vanna Restaurant & Bar

Vanna Restaurant & Bar is a cozy riverfront restaurant in Kampot known for its relaxed atmosphere and flavorful mix of Khmer, fusion, and Western cuisine. Popular dishes include smoked duck lok lak, sweet mango snake fish, rosemary and lime salmon, pomelo chicken salad, saraman, and Kampot green pepper squid. The restaurant is vegetarian-friendly, offers vegan options, and is praised for its welcoming service and scenic waterfront setting.

Cambodian Taste

Traditional Food

Kampot Pepper Crab (or Seafood with Kampot Pepper)

One of the most iconic dishes in Kampot (and nearby Kep) is crab cooked with fresh green Kampot pepper. The pepper is world-famous for its strong aroma and slightly spicy flavor, making this dish a must-try for seafood lovers.

Kampot Green Pepper Squid

Kampot Green Pepper Squid is a signature Cambodian stir-fry featuring fresh squid, aromatic green Kampot peppercorns, and savory sauces. It is typically made by wok-tossing squid with fresh green peppercorns, garlic, onion, fish sauce, oyster sauce, and coconut milk for a savory, slightly spicy dish.

Num Banh Chok Teuk Mhech

Num Banh Chok Teuk Mhech, also known as Kampot’s iconic noodle soup, is a traditional dish that beautifully captures the essence of local cuisine. It begins with fresh rice noodles made from finely ground rice flour, offering a soft and delicate texture that pairs perfectly with its rich, aromatic broth. The broth is infused with classic Khmer ingredients such as lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and a touch of fish sauce, creating a fragrant and flavorful base. What makes this dish truly special is its vibrant topping of fresh herbs and vegetables, including basil, cucumber, and banana flower, often served with Kampot fish.

Crab Fried Rice

Crab Fried Rice is a beloved dish that showcases Kampot’s abundant seafood and vibrant flavors.The dish starts with fragrant jasmine rice, cooked to perfection and infused with the subtle aroma of pandan leaves. What sets Crab Fried Rice apart is its simplicity and elegance – the dish allows the natural sweetness of the crab meat to shine, complemented by the savory flavors of the fried rice. It’s a dish that’s both comforting and sophisticated, perfect for savoring with friends and family.

Sweet Noodles Wrap

Delicate rice noodles are wrapped around a medley of fresh, vibrant ingredients, including shredded coconut, palm sugar, and sesame seed. Also, each bite offers a symphony of flavors and textures, with the softness of the noodles contrasting perfectly with the crunch of the coconut and the rich sweetness of the palm sugar. It’s a culinary masterpiece that’s sure to tantalize your taste buds and leave you craving more.
